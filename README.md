# Fisko

Fisko is a proposed agency e-budgeting platform for Thai government budget units (system name: AEBMS, Agency e-Budgeting Management System). It covers the full budget cycle, from strategic planning and budget requests through review, submission to the Budget Bureau, allocation, execution monitoring and performance, with AI assistance throughout.

This repository holds the conceptual design material and a clickable prototype prepared by Avalant for customer discussions. All figures, people and projects in the prototype are fictional sample data.

## Prototype

- All 10 modules: M01 Strategic Planning, M02 Budget Preparation, M03 Consolidation, M04 Review & Approval, M05 Submission, M06 Allocation, M07 Execution Monitoring, M08 Performance, M09 Budget Intelligence (AI), M10 System Administration.
- Eight roles (division, planning, executive, finance, auditor, administrator, procurement officer, head of procurement), Thai/English, light and dark themes.
- Deeper module features, built in rounds and listed in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md). Round 8 added the M01 project bank and plan revision, the M02 standard-price catalogue and paste from Excel, and the M03 ceiling responses and 4-year forward estimates. Round 9 added M04 budget hearings and cut impact, M05 parliament stages, and M06 allotment releases and carry-over. Round 10 added the M07 year-end forecast and procurement milestones, M08 project evaluation (lessons flow to the M01 project bank), M09 spending anomalies and justification quality, and M10 notification templates, SLA escalation, retention and backup.
- Round 11 added light versions of **Fisko Procurement** (procurement plan, requisitions, contracts with acceptance phases, e-GP file import) and **Fisko Finance** (allocation-to-payment chain, payment approvals, GFMIS file reconciliation), plus duty-separation rules (M10 → Duty separation). They are built on the FY2570 allocation lines and use files only for e-GP and GFMIS.
- Round 13 built P0 and P1 of the [Procurement design](Fisko_Procurement_Design.html): a **procurement case file** with 12 steps (method advice from a rules table in M10, head-of-procurement approval of overrides, committees with checks, reference price, e-GP details, bidders, award, appeal window, contract gate), a split-purchase check on requisitions, a new duty rule SD-06, and an event timeline per case. Appeal-period and holiday rules are settings in M10 → Procurement rules; legal thresholds other than the few confirmed ones are marked as to be confirmed.
- Round 14 built P2 of the Procurement design: contract guarantee with expiry warnings, delivery notices and acceptance deadlines, penalties for late delivery, acceptance with partial results and recorded dissent, contract changes (amount or time) approved by a ladder with new contract versions, a **three-way payment gate** (acceptance, invoice, guarantee) in front of Finance with exception release, and closing the case. New duty rule SD-08. Rates and ladders are settings in M10 → Procurement rules.
- Round 15 built P3 of the Procurement design: an **Analytics** tab (case funnel, KPI-01 to KPI-14 defined once, time in each step), a **Red flags** tab (RF-01 to RF-10 with scores, evidence and a triage workflow: ask, explain, decide, count false positives) and an **OCDS 1.1 export** of published data only (JSON shown on the page, with copy and download). Thresholds are settings in M10 → Procurement rules.
- Round 16 built P4 of the Procurement design: an **AI assistant** tab with AI-01 (consolidation and split watch), AI-02/11 (document drafts with edit tracking), AI-03 (reference price with evidence), AI-04 (TOR spec-lock and completeness check), AI-05 (method advice), AI-07 (late-delivery risk), AI-09 (questions answered from the KPI layer) and AI-10 (supplier record summary). It uses rules and statistics only, logs every use in M09, respects the M09 agent switches (new agents AG-07, AG-08, AG-10), and declines to score bids, pick suppliers, announce winners or approve.
- Round 18 built Q1 of design v0.2 (sourcing in depth): a **TOR library** with versions approved by the head of procurement, market research per case, **conflict-of-interest declarations** with duty rule SD-07 and member replacement, published **evaluation criteria** and a scoring matrix where committee members enter their own scores and the system only adds them up, Q&A and amendments during the announcement, an e-GP data pack, and a warning when the e-GP file is ahead of the case.
- Round 19 built Q2 (contract late stage and suppliers): supervisor weekly reports and 50% / 75% / end checkpoints, termination approved by the executive (guarantee forfeited, open phases cancelled, commitment reduced), warranty period and defect claims, letters to suppliers from templates with sent dates, a **supplier register** with tax-ID check and history, and the **abandoned-work list** check before award and contract. The Procurement and Finance pages no longer repeat their menu as a tab row; the left menu is the only navigation.
- Round 20 built Q3 (plan and reports): procurement **categories**, **plan versions** proposed by the officer, approved by the executive and recorded as published in e-GP (requisitions only for items in the published plan), demand intake from M06 lines and next year's M02 requests, the **monthly procurement summary** in the สขร.1 layout with gap checks and publishing, an **audit dossier** per case, and a **timeline (Gantt)** of every case against the fiscal year.
- Round 21 built Q4 (document intake, simulated OCR): sample invoices, delivery notes, bank guarantees and quotations are "read" with a confidence per field (one field misread on purpose); checks V-01 to V-07 run for real; a person verifies low-confidence fields and fixes errors before confirming; confirmed data feeds the payment gate, delivery dates, the guarantee register and case quotations. Uploaded files open as an empty form. Registered as agent AG-14 in M09.
- Round 22 built Q5 (forecast and what-if): a **Forecast** page (F-01 monthly commitments and payments with P10–P90, F-02 chance each open case signs before a chosen cut-off, F-03 price trend by category, F-04 carry-over risk with a notice to Finance, F-05 officer workload), a **What-if** page with levers W-01 to W-06 plus the signing cut-off, compared side by side and saved as scenarios (real data never changes), forecast markers on the case timeline, and dashboard cards for each role on the Analytics page.
- Round 23 built Q6 (agentic AI, simulated): an **Agents** page where Case Copilot (AG-11), Contract Watch (AG-12), Compliance Reporter (AG-13) and a multi-step Insight agent (AG-10) take a goal, plan steps from a template, call tools from a register, and stop for a person at every write, one step at a time. Autonomy levels L0 to L3 are set by the executive with a reason. Steps, timing and approvals are traced and logged. With this round, rounds P0 to P4 and Q1 to Q6 of the Procurement design are all in the prototype; the demo guide has 25 steps.
- Round 24 refreshed the whole interface (design system v2, "clean enterprise"): navy-tinted neutrals around the navy and green brand, Inter and Anuphan type, soft elevation, an icon menu, a lighter top bar, stat cards with sparklines and progress rings, a redesigned **My Workspace** (hero, cross-app stats, filterable tasks, calendar strip, app tiles), a **pipeline board** for procurement cases, a case page with a progress ring, step track and pinned next-step panel, and phase tracks on contracts. Every other screen picks up the new look through shared styles.
- Round 25 redesigned the **document review** screen (four-step progress strip, sticky document image linked to each field on hover, a confidence meter per field, check chips and an action bar that says what is left), and gave the **Budget module** the new look: an icon on every page heading, KPI tiles with icons and colour accents, segmented tabs, a status **board for budget requests** (M02) and a **round-by-round visual** for review and approval (M04).
- Round 26 made the look **calmer**: one slate-navy accent, status colours only as small dots, grey icons, flat surfaces with thin borders, semibold type, a light sidebar and no motion (`src/calm.css`). Charts keep the Fisko colours, led by the brand green.
- Round 27 added **AI co-work**: a Claude/ChatGPT-style chat in every module. Open it from the **AI** button in the top bar (or Ctrl+J) as a side panel that knows the page and record you are on, or full screen with conversation history and an **agent directory**. Talk to the general Fisko Assistant, which routes the question, or pick a specialist agent (budget AG-01 to 06, procurement AG-07 to 14) with the picker or `@AG-05`. Attach files (picker, drag-and-drop, paste: CSV and text are really read; images and PDFs get a simulated reading), attach Fisko records, or speak (browser speech recognition, simulated when the microphone is blocked). Answers use the rules and sample data, show the steps taken, and offer buttons; the agents never approve, change amounts, sign, pay or pick suppliers.
- Round 28 built **B1** of the [Budget design](Fisko_Budget_Design.html): one data spine and a step-by-step request form. Requests have numbered steps with a status dot each and a "next" hint: lines and justification, a **quarterly plan** (forms สงป.301/302, filled from budget-type patterns), **readiness and risks** (site, staff, management, materials, six risk areas, evaluation), and **documents required by condition** (specification, drawings, three quotations, CIO or committee for ICT, corruption-risk assessment, Cabinet resolution), plus location, GPS and price source for investment lines. New rules BR-07 to BR-13. A new **division director** role approves before Planning; Planning sends to the committee only above the threshold or for new projects, otherwise to the executive. Consolidation totals and the review rounds are now computed from the requests, with a 20% cap check and an amount **waterfall**.
- Round 29 built **B2**: M01 has a **link to national plans** tree (national strategy → master plan → agency strategy → KPI → requests, with amounts asked and decided, requests without a KPI and KPIs without a request) and minimum budget shares per strategy set by the executive. M03 ranks projects on the Budget Bureau's **five criteria** (obligation and readiness filled from the request), adds a **3R analysis** (Review last year's spending and carry-overs, Redeploy from low-score or slow-spending requests, Replace into high-score requests below the line, usable as Planning's round-2 proposal), a **zero-based view** (new, continuing and rolled-over requests; Planning can ask a division to explain a request again, and the division answers on the request) and, in forward estimates, how much of each future year is **already locked**.
- Round 30 built **B3**: a **committee member** role and a committee (chair, members, non-voting secretary, quorum of half). Meetings record attendance and block resolutions without a quorum, members vote before the meeting, members from the requesting unit abstain (SD-10), and **minutes are drafted** from the record. A cut shows its **impact** on KPI targets and the quarterly plan before it is resolved, and on the request afterwards. M05 gets the **minister's approval** (section 25) as a gate before sending, a **submission pack** in the Budget Bureau report layout with a CSV for e-Budgeting, and a **briefing pack** (last year's scrutiny observations, one page per request, likely questions, AI-drafted answers by AG-16). M06 creates the FY2571 **allocation lines from the appropriation act** (after parliament's cuts and restored appeals), each linked to its request, with monthly plans from the request's quarterly plan.
- Round 31 built **B4** (live in-year management): **four-level balances** per FY2570 line (allotted, reserved from requisitions, committed from contracts, paid from Finance; available = allotted − reserved − committed) with a drill-down; allotment status and the 15-day hand-on to regional offices; **transfers classified by the regulation** (T1 within an output with a 15-day report clock, T2 and T3 needing the Budget Bureau after the executive, T4 needing Cabinet, T6 extended money) with the five reasons of clause 25; **spending-plan versions** with the clause-15 reasons and the "15 days before the year" check; **savings** from contracts below the estimate that can be kept or turned into a transfer; the carry-over rule (section 43); and a **live monitoring** tab in M07 for FY2570 against quarterly targets that are yearly settings, including investment not yet committed.
- Round 32 built **B5** (results, M08): **quarterly reports** per unit for each round of FY2569 (Q1 to Q4 due 15 days after the quarter, the annual report 45 days after the year) with results, problems, fixes and **evidence** the auditor certifies, and Planning accepts or returns them; **cost per output** (actual, planned and last year, the 3E view); **evaluation at three moments** plus impact (before funding from the request's readiness, during from spending, output and reports, after from the project evaluation); a register of **recommendations to act on** with deadlines (section 48); and a **public summary page** that Planning or the executive approves for publication (section 47).
- Round 33 built **B6** (AI and analytics, M09), completing the Budget design: **budget agents** that run as multi-step jobs and stop for a person at every step that changes data or notifies people (AG-02 checks every request waiting for Planning, AG-03 runs 3R into round 2, AG-04 proposes three sets of requests under the ceiling, AG-05 watches this year's spending and creates tasks for units at risk, AG-15 finds money for a line and files a transfer, AG-17 collects quarterly reports and chases late units); a **year-end forecast** as a range against the target, likely carry-over and next year's locked amount; **four what-ifs** (ceiling cut by rank, proportion or protecting commitments; an investment line slipping a quarter; moving savings; a scrutiny cut with a drafted appeal); and **KPI-B01 to 10** with **warning signals BF-01 to 06**. AG-15 to 17 are also available in the AI co-work chat.
- Round 35 extended the **demo guide** to 39 steps (the Budget cycle in depth, steps 26 to 39, in the app and the presenter script), brought the **Budget design to v0.2**, and added a **test runner** (`tests/run.html`).
- Round 34 tidied two things left from B6: tasks that agents create for a division now reach only that division (and its director), and a new **M10 tab "Budget AI and signals"** holds the yearly spending targets, the forecast assumptions, the warning-signal thresholds and the 3R rules (administrator edits; finance and the auditor read; every change is audited).
- An **app switcher** (click the logo at the top of the menu) moves between Fisko Budget, Fisko Procurement and Fisko Finance. Each app shows only its own menu; My Workspace and System Administration are shared.
- Simulated SSO sign-in with two-factor codes (the prototype code is `246810`), global search (Ctrl+K or `/`), a change-history drawer on records, and delegation of authority (acting on another user's behalf, recorded in the audit log).
- Two sample organizations: a fictional department, and กรมโยธาธิการและผังเมือง with fictional figures and an indicative unit structure.
- State is kept in the browser's local storage. Use **Reset data** or the **Demo guide** (bottom of the left menu) to start over.

### Build

The prototype is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no dependencies. Edit the files in `src/`, then run:

```bash
bash src/build.sh
```

This regenerates `Fisko_Prototype.html` and `fisko-prototype.html`.
